About Paintline

A ball, a board and tiles waiting for colour. Paintline is a small, independent puzzle game about the joy of clear thinking.

The idea

Paintline started from a simple question: how much thinking can fit into a single swipe? The rule takes five seconds to learn, the ball slides until it stops, but the boards grow into real logic puzzles. Every level is checked by a solver so that its target is the true shortest solution, never a guess.

A story told in colour

A grey fog called Pallor has drained the colours from the Land of Colours. You guide Damla, the smallest drop of paint, from dusty workshops to coral coves, night gardens, glaciers and windy valleys. In each world you meet a new friend, such as Captain Ink, the eight-armed sailor, or Tick the Clockmaker, and each one brings a new puzzle idea with them. Painting a world's tiles is how its colour comes back.

Where you can play

  • In the browser: paintline.lol runs on phones, tablets and computers. It can be added to the home screen and works offline after the first visit.
  • On iPhone and iPad: from the App Store.
  • On Android: a Google Play version is in testing.

The game is available in English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Russian, Arabic and Chinese.

Frequently asked questions

Is Paintline free?

Yes. All levels and modes can be played for free. Coins are earned by playing: finishing a level for the first time with three stars without hints or powers, daily tasks, chests and the weekly tournament. They buy cosmetic balls, trails and avatars, plus one-use powers.

Do I need an account?

No. Progress is saved on your device. If you sign in with Google or with email, your progress and settings are kept in your account and follow you to other devices.

Does it work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the game opens and the story levels play without a connection. Online features such as the weekly tournament, leaderboards and friend challenges need the internet.

Are there ads?

The browser version may offer an optional video that you can choose to watch for extra undos. Ads never interrupt a level. The iPhone app has no ads.

Is it suitable for children?

Paintline contains no violence and no chat. Player names on leaderboards can be reported or hidden.

What data do you collect?

As little as possible. The privacy policy lists exactly what is stored and how to delete it.

I found a bug or have an idea.

We read every message. The support page has answers to common problems and our contact address.
